SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY — replace with an approved business policy.

Receiving goods
Compare deliveries with the approved purchase draft. Check quantity, packaging,
expiry and unit cost. Record physical receipt only after this check.

Replenishment
Review milk, eggs and bread daily. Review pantry goods each Monday.
Historical sales imports do not update physical stock. Confirm stock counts first.

Pricing
An owner or manager must approve proposed price changes. Staff must confirm the
approved price before applying it to the local catalog. Keep the agreed margin floor.

Evidence
Check source dates and product variants. A popular search result is not proof of
local sales. New products start with a budgeted trial and a sell-through review.
